How a Motor Claim Journey Works

A clear claim journey helps customers know what information may be requested and what stage comes next.

Motor Insurance · Educational article

A clear claim journey helps customers know what information may be requested and what stage comes next.

Notify the insurer

Use the official claim channel shown in the policy or insurer communication and provide accurate incident details.

Follow document requests

The insurer may request information relevant to assessment. Keep copies of communications and submitted documents.

Track the assessment

Inspection, repair authorisation or review steps depend on the circumstances and applicable policy terms.

Review the outcome

Read the insurer communication carefully and use official support or grievance channels for questions.
